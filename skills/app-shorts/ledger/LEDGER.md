# app-shorts — ledger

The skill's own record: versions, lessons and runs that taught something. Append rows; never rewrite them.
Per-episode facts live in the registry (`/Volumes/ssd/projects/app-shorts/registry.json`); per-app notes in the
app's repository; each run in the owner's activity ledger.

## Lessons

- 2026-11-04: The channel's best Shorts before this skill were a 7 s calm alphabet loop (about 1.3K views at day 3,
  207% average viewed) and plain "Search Newsworthy in the App Store" clips (1.1K and 250); its feature demos and
  "coming soon" clips sat at 1–88. Short, loopable and simple beat long and explanatory.
- 2026-11-04: Chrome's `file_upload` only reads the session's folders; Studio's schedule time field ignores
  typing; the captions and thumbnail uploaders have hidden file inputs that take files without opening a picker.
- 2026-11-04: Uploading an `.srt` that repeats burned-in captions shows the text twice for viewers with captions on.
- 2026-11-12: Chrome's CDP screencast (`Page.startScreencast`) delivers frames at CSS pixels (390x844 for a 390-wide
  viewport) whatever the device scale factor, so a cut built from it is a 2.8x upscale and reviewers call it soft. For a
  web build, install the page clock (`page.clock.install`) and step it, taking a `page.screenshot` per frame: true 3x
  frames (1170x2532), deterministic timing (Data Center dc-first-01).
- 2026-11-12: An Apple Loop's name says nothing about gaps. Bedlam Synth Layers and Airy Vox Synth drop below -70 dB
  for 50 ms windows; measure each loop with `astats` at 50 ms (`asetnsamples=2400`) before building a bed on it.
- 2026-11-12: Newsworthy's front page names real people in real national stories (today: 5/10 on a CEO's
  resignation). Show it whole, as the app shows it (2.11.0; the earlier "keep it out of frame" lesson was wrong). Pick
  `EB6GGrACO10` (the B film), not `54ZNifKYxtk`, as the October reference: the registry's legacy rows carry no file or
  description, so match a legacy Short to its source by its public thumbnail (`i.ytimg.com/vi/<id>/oar2.jpg`).
- 2026-11-12: A simulator take's screen carries the status bar and the Dynamic Island, so a "screens only" cut still shows
  Apple hardware. Clip the top 180 px of a 1320x2868 iPhone 16 Pro Max frame (status bar and island) and keep the
  image in place under the clip, so tap marks stay on their targets (importron-calm-v5c).
- 2026-11-12: `shorts.mjs phrase` re-checks the profile's verified phrase live; one App Store 429 on it makes it return
  a weaker candidate ("Importron", rank 2) instead of the profile's "Importron health" (rank 1, checked that day).
  Trust the profile's `searchPhrase` when the store refuses the check; `record` enforces the profile's phrase anyway.
- 2026-11-13: Headless Chromium will not lay a page out narrower than 500 CSS px (a 393 px viewport reports
  `innerWidth` 500 and the right edge is cut off). Record a phone-shaped web take at 540x1170 or wider.
- 2026-11-13: `taskpolicy -b` (background QoS) slowed a headless Chromium recording about tenfold (295 frames in four
  minutes); the same take at `nice -n 10` and screenshot scale 1 ran 2,490 frames in 126 s. Keep `taskpolicy -b` for
  ffmpeg renders, not for recordings that must finish.
- 2026-11-13: The October Newsworthy Shorts, the only ones the owner says sold the app, had empty descriptions on
  YouTube; `yt-api.mjs update` refused a description-only fix because their old titles fail the 40-character rule.
  A description-only update now keeps the old title.
- 2026-11-13: E1 and E5 both name "the next Space Worms Short" (E5 at 7 to 10 s), while the file says each new Short
  sits in one arm of one experiment. sw-e1-01 was kept at 11 s so it is only in E1; E5's Space Worms arm needs its own
  Short. The same clash exists for Newsworthy between E4 and any other arm.
- 2026-11-13: `thumbnails.set` returned 403 on a fresh Short (sw-e1-01); its cover stays YouTube's pick.
- 2026-11-13: one Short, one content experiment (E1, E3, E4, E5); E2 (when the comment and Related video go up) runs
  across them by order of posting, 1st A, 2nd B, and so on. The Newsworthy E4 Short and the Space Worms E5 Short were
  each kept out of every other content arm.
- 2026-11-13: an ffmpeg `vstack` of a 1080x2 strip scaled to a band gave newsworthy-loop-01 a sample aspect ratio of
  1994213:30672. YouTube drew it upright but classed it a regular video, not a Short (`/shorts/` kept answering 303
  after processing). It was made private and re-uploaded with `setsar=1` as 01b, which YouTube classed as a Short at
  once. `yt-api.mjs upload` now refuses a file with non-square pixels.
- 2026-11-13: a 403 from `thumbnails.set` right after upload is a timing error. `yt-api.mjs thumbnail --episode ID
  --confirm` set sw-e1-01's cover later with a 200.
- 2026-11-13: `shorts.mjs phrase vegas-microworks` returned "Vegas Microworks, on the App Store." for a business with no
  app. A business with a registry `site` now ends on that address.
- 2026-11-13: production notes had crept back into descriptions despite the 2.5.0 rule ("Staged in an offline round",
  "Filmed in the real app with a made-up name", "the iPhone status bar is drawn in", "sped up"). Cut from 13 live
  YouTube descriptions and 3 TikTok captions (sw-giant-e5-01, sw-e1-01, sw-triple-01); `findability` now flags them.
  sw-giant-e5-01 still opens on the burned-in caption "Space Worms · offline vs bots"; only a re-upload removes it.
