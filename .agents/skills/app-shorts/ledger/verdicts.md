# The owner's verdicts on Shorts

Every reviewer reads this file (`shorts.mjs rules <app>` prints it). It is the owner's taste on record: what he
rejected and why, and what worked. A new Short that repeats a rejected pattern fails review. Append a row whenever the
owner gives a verdict or pulls a Short (the registry's `pulledReason`); never rewrite one.
