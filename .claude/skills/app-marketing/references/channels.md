# Channels

What each channel is for, and whether an unattended run may use it. Evidence first: use what has moved a number. Every
released app works the open channels without asking; a new app needs no go-ahead for any of them.

## Open

| Channel | Runner | Why it earns a place |
|---|---|---|
| YouTube Shorts | mac | The only channel with proven reach so far: 1,100 to 1,300 views on the best Shorts. Series on the owner's channel, by `app-shorts` |
| App Store search | mac | Keywords, subtitle and promotional text, where most downloads of an unknown app begin. Keywords ride the next version; promotional text changes any time |
| Featuring nominations | mac | Free, and indie apps are eligible. Three weeks' notice, for real updates only |
| Custom product pages | mac | A page per audience, once a channel sends enough traffic to matter |
| Review replies | mac | Factual and brief. They show on the product page |
| YouTube comment replies and channel settings | mac | The owner's own channel, by `youtube-growth` and `app-shorts`. Replies are factual and brief; a settings change logs the old value |
| Search guides | cloud | Compounding traffic from people already looking. Slow: judge at 8 weeks, not 8 days |
| llms.txt and structured data | cloud | What assistants read when someone asks what to play or install. Generated, so it stays true |
| Press page | cloud | Inbound only: makes it easy for anyone who finds the app to write about it |

## Closed until the owner opens them

Each of these publishes as the owner to people who did not ask, or spends money. An unattended run does not use them or
wait for them. To open one, the owner says so in chat, naming the channel; record the words and date here.

| Channel | What it would need |
|---|---|
| Press and creator pitches by email | Permission to email chosen recipients from the owner's address |
| Reddit, Hacker News, Product Hunt, TikTok, Instagram, X, Discord | An account the owner already has, and permission to post as them. Never create one |
| Game directories and web portals | Permission to submit forms, and an existing account where one is required |
| Apple Ads | A monthly cap |
| Free weekend (Newsworthy) | The owner's decision on the price |
