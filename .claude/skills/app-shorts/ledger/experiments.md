What the five taught (read before the next plan; importron-easy-v3 added below):

- **The middle is where they all lose.** Every reviewer marked down the stretch where the real app plays as a
  framed screen recording under a caption: small UI, holds, the same shape beat after beat. Openings and endings
  scored well. Next time, make the app's moment the spectacle: full-bleed, the UI pulled apart and animated, the
  character inside the app, or one hero result held big, never a small phone of tiny rows.
- **Disco Funk was picked three times out of five.** Genre choice converged; choose the genre last and differently.
- **No numbers yet.** None has been posted, so nothing here is evidence of what the audience likes.
- **importron-easy-v3 (panel-gated).** Clear and true from frame 0 ("says what it is" 8 from all three) and plainly an
  iPhone, but every reviewer called the step-pill-plus-device-mockup-plus-stock-loop format a template, and the payoff
  (a Health list) earned no share. Softening an app's own small print in post was itself flagged by the skeptic. Next:
  a payoff only Health can show (a trend from two dated imports), one unbroken camera, sound with character.

**The panel does not measure taste (2026-11-12).** After 13 attempts and no pass, the panel's final scores were
compared with the owner's own verdicts on three cuts. It ranked them in reverse: the Space Worms documentary
("font story sound all bad") scored highest (6 8 7 7 7 6 8 6), the Importron robot ("ok", later "horrible")
middle, and Füd one-day v1 ("ok") lowest (4 6 6 6 7 5 7 5). Line 1, "stops the scroll", sat at 4 to 7 in every
round of every attempt. The panel reliably finds concrete faults (pricing leaks, glitch frames, unreadable words,
untrue claims, hard-to-follow footage). It is not evidence of what viewers or the owner will like.

**Vegas Microworks free-tool series (2026-11-12 weekly pass).** Eight Shorts, one per free tool or guide, made in
parallel by separate builders from one brief with a look, typeface and loop folder assigned to each so none repeat.
One independent reviewer per batch (three lenses and hard gates in one agent, not three agents) found real faults in
every cut; all named faults were fixed before upload. What it taught: dark green with gold or a vignette under the
word "Vegas" reads as a card table (two cuts recoloured); every cut opened on a calm static frame and scored 3 to 6
on "stops the scroll", so the next Vegas Microworks Short should open mid-motion on a result; nobody listened to
any mix, they were only measured.
