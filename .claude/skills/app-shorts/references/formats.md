# Series formats

A format is a repeatable shape: the same hook pattern, a new real input each episode, one payoff. Pick one or two
per app, record them in the registry (`apps.<slug>.formats`) and let `youtube-growth` keep or drop them on the
numbers. Each line below is a starting point, not a script.

## Shapes that work for apps

| Shape | Hook (first second, as text) | Payoff | Fits |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Can the app do this?** | "Can an app count the calories in *this*?" | The real result, punched in | Any AI or smart feature |
| **One number** | "Today's biggest story, rated" | The number, big | Scores, ratings, totals |
| **Satisfying loop** | none, or one word | A calm, perfect loop that ends where it began | Kids' games, ambient apps |
| **Before → after** | "A lab PDF → Apple Health" | The transformed result | Converters, organisers |
| **Comment-driven** | "You asked: …" (quote a real comment) | The app answering it | Any format, from episode 2 |
| **Challenge** | "Can you beat 30 seconds?" | The near-miss or the win | Games |
| **Parent tip** | One concrete tip ("Say /s/ like a snake") | The app doing it with the child | Education, speech practice |

## Per app (seeds; check the app's current features before using one)

- **Füd** (family food tracker, describe a meal): "Can Füd work out this meal?" Use relatable parent meals
  (crusts, leftovers, dinner for three) and comment-supplied meals. Live as series 1.
- **Giraffe Stretch** (letter game for little learners): a 7–10 s calm loop per letter ("Calm and cute ABCs"),
  the channel's best performer so far. It was not on sale on 2026-11-12: no new Short until it is (`SKILL.md`, Rules).
- **Pipsqueak** (speech and language practice for children): one-sound parent tips with the app modelling the sound;
  never imply therapy outcomes.
- **Nametag** (badges for parent meetups): "Which badge would you wear?" (a quick build, with the choice asked in
  the comments).
- **Newsworthy** (world news rated 1–10): "Today's biggest story, rated", with the app's own score and one line, on
  the day. Accuracy matters more than speed.
- **Data Center** (pixel strategy game): a valley growing in time-lapse; "Can it survive the night?"
- **Rhythm** (an endless place to slow down): 10-second calm loops; sound is the content, so use the app's own audio.
- **Importron** (lab PDF → Apple Health): before and after with a synthetic lab report only, never real results.
- **Space Worms** (game): near-misses and wins, "Can you beat this level?"

## Rules

- **Only for apps on sale.** A seed for an app not on sale waits; there is no "coming soon" format.
- **One idea per Short, 10–20 seconds**, with the hook on screen in the first second.
- **Show what the product can do.** Staged, scripted and edited footage is fine; a feature it doesn't have is not.
- **Vary the input, keep the shape:** that is what makes a series rather than "repetitious content" (see
  `youtube-growth/references/policy.md`).
- **Kids appear only as app users' names or emoji,** never as filmed children. Most formats speak to parents
  and players. Every Short is set "not made for kids" (`youtube-growth/references/policy.md`).
